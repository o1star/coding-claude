Refund Policy

Admission

Entry to VWCC shows is free, so there is nothing to refund for attendees.

Vendor table refunds

Vendors may request a refund up to two (2) weeks (14 days) before the event date. Refund requests made within 14 days of the event are not eligible for a refund.

This policy helps us finalize floor plans, staffing, and event logistics.

How to request a refund

Email us at vancouverwcc@gmail.com with your vendor name, the event date, and the number of tables booked. Requests are dated by when we receive your email. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method, normally within 10 business days.

Transferring a table

If you can't attend, you may ask to transfer your table to another vendor. Transfers must be approved by VWCC in advance, and the new vendor must agree to our Terms & Conditions.

No-shows

Vendors who do not show up on the event day without cancelling at least 14 days in advance are not eligible for a refund.

If we cancel or reschedule

If VWCC cancels a show, vendors receive a full refund of their table fees. If a show is rescheduled, vendors may move their booking to the new date or request a full refund.

If a show must be cancelled because of events outside our control, such as severe weather, venue closures, or public health orders, we will offer a credit toward a future show or a refund, at our discretion.

Removal from a show

Vendors removed from a show for breaking our Terms & Conditions are not eligible for a refund.

Privacy Policy

Vancouver Westside Collectibles Convention ("VWCC", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through vwcc.ca and at our shows, how we use it, and your choices. We follow British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

Information we collect

  • Vendor applications: your name, email, phone number, business or vendor name, social media handle, number of tables, and any notes you send us.
  • Contact messages: your name, email, and message.
  • Event sign-ups: your name and email if you RSVP or join our mailing list.
  • Payments: table payments are processed by our payment providers. We do not store your full card details.
  • Website data: our website host collects basic technical information such as your browser type, device, pages visited, and approximate location, using cookies and similar tools.

How we use it

  • To review vendor applications, assign tables, and send invoices and event details
  • To answer your questions
  • To send show announcements, if you've signed up for them
  • To run giveaways and contact winners
  • To keep our website working and understand how it's used

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers who help us run VWCC, such as our website host (Wix), payment processors, and email tools, and only as needed for those services. We may also share information if required by law.

Photos and video at our shows

We take photos and video at our events and may post them on our website and social media. If you'd prefer not to appear, let a staff member know at the show, or email us and we'll remove a photo of you.

Email updates

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any email or by contacting us.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as required for tax and accounting records.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email us at vancouverwcc@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to keep it working and to measure traffic. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work properly without them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email vancouverwcc@gmail.com.

Giveaways

Photos and video

Accessibility Statement

Vancouver Westside Collectibles Convention ("VWCC") wants everyone to be able to enjoy our shows and our website, including people with disabilities.

Our website

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. This website:

  • Works with keyboard navigation and screen readers
  • Uses clear headings, readable text sizes, and strong colour contrast
  • Includes text descriptions for meaningful images
  • Reduces motion for visitors who have turned on "reduce motion" on their device
  • Works on phones, tablets, and computers

At our shows

Accessibility features such as entrances, washrooms, and parking depend on each venue. If you have questions or need help planning your visit, please contact us before the show and we'll do our best to help. Service animals are welcome.

Known limitations

Some content, such as event photos and social media posts from other platforms, may not be fully accessible. We are working to improve this.

Feedback

If you have trouble using any part of our website or have suggestions, please email vancouverwcc@gmail.com. We'll respond as soon as we can, and we'll try to provide the information you need in another format.
