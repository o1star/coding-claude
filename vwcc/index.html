FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else? Send us a message.

Is it really free to get in?

Yes. VWCC is free admission for everyone at every show.

What can I find there?

Pokémon, sports cards, One Piece, Magic and other TCGs, plus collectibles. Vendors sell singles, graded cards and sealed product.

Can I sell or trade my cards?

Yes. Lots of vendors buy and trade on the floor, so bring your binder. If you have a lot to move, book a table.

How do the giveaways work?

We run giveaways every hour while the show is open. Be there to win.

How do I book a vendor table?

Email or text us with what you sell and how many tables you need. Watch our Instagram for vendor announcements.
