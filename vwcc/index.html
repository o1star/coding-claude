FAQ

How much does it cost to get in?

Admission is . Check back here before each show for the latest info.

Can I sell or trade my own cards?

Yes. Many vendors buy and trade on the floor, so bring your cards. If you want to sell a lot, consider booking a table.

Do vendors take cards or e-transfer?

Most vendors take cash and many take Interac e-Transfer or card. Cash is always a safe bet.

Is it kid-friendly?

Absolutely. Card collecting is for all ages, and Pokémon fans of every age are welcome.

How do I become a vendor?

Email us with your name, what you sell, and how many tables you need. See the Vendors section for details.
